What I Do

My superpower? I'm a commercial catalyst who thrives on turning "what if" into "we did it". Whether it's scaling a business from 120 to 500+ staff, negotiating seven-figure enterprise deals, or steering companies through complex M&A transactions, I bring both strategic vision and tactical execution to the table.

My Operating Model

I'm driven by what I call GROWTH: Goal-oriented. Results-focused. Opportunity-driven. Winning mindset. Team-first. Human-centred.

This combination is about having an achievement mindset and a bias for action whilst also being profoundly self-reflective and tuned in to the people around me. Because at the end of the day, it always comes down to people.

I'm naturally curious and have a knack for quickly connecting the dots. My roles have always focused on translating business opportunities into scalable strategies that drive efficiency, performance, and revenue growth. I oversee commercial operations, customer acquisition, and strategic partnerships across the continuum of today's business needs.
