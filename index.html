Estate & Asset Commercialisation

Alongside the technology work, I help universities and public-sector organisations make money from what they already own. Many sit on large estates and valuable assets but have leaned on a single income line, usually student accommodation, and have never built the commercial engine to release the rest of the value.

I come in as an owner and operator, not a consultant looking in from the outside. Through Adams Property Limited I run a portfolio of 45 residential properties alongside selected commercial holdings, so I read estate value the way an investor does. I have advised universities and public bodies on partnerships, revenue strategy, and go-to-market for fundraising, taking early-stage assets and turning them into a plan that generates income.

How I work with boards and estates teams

I sit down with the people who hold the decisions, cut through the noise, and tell them plainly what to do to commercialise the estate. Two to a few days a month, focused on outcomes, not process. The result is a clear commercial plan the team can act on, with the numbers attached.
